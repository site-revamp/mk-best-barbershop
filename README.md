# MK Barber Shop – Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

MK Barber Shop is a premium men's grooming business established in 2016 in Dubai. It offers professional haircuts, skin fades, beard styling, traditional shaving and personalised grooming services across three UAE locations.

The redesigned homepage positions MK Barber Shop as a premium grooming destination, highlighting its barbering expertise, service offerings, branch locations and appointment options. It targets modern gentlemen, professionals and customers seeking quality men's grooming.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Booking Journey

- Improved the visibility of appointment booking actions.
- Made branch-specific booking options easier to discover.
- Provided clearer pathways to the existing Fresha booking platform.

**Problem addressed:** The previous website relied on an external booking platform without a sufficiently prominent booking journey, adding unnecessary steps for customers.

### 2. Better Service and Pricing Visibility

- Organised grooming services into clearer categories.
- Improved the presentation of haircuts, fades, beard styling and shaving services.
- Established a clearer structure for service and pricing information.

**Problem addressed:** Customers could not easily explore a complete service menu or compare prices from the homepage.

### 3. Improved Local SEO and Branch Information

- Strengthened the presentation of the three Dubai branch locations.
- Improved the organisation of business contact information.
- Addressed the need for complete branch-specific LocalBusiness or BarberShop structured data, including addresses, telephone numbers and opening hours.

**Problem addressed:** Incomplete structured business information made it harder for search engines to interpret the business and its individual locations consistently.

## Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Reinforced the premium men's grooming identity through a more refined and professional appearance.
- **Layout:** Improved the organisation of service categories, booking actions and branch information.
- **User Experience:** Created clearer navigation between grooming services, locations and appointment options.
- **Brand Consistency:** Maintained a cohesive presentation of the business and its three locations.
- **Conversion Focus:** Gave greater prominence to booking and contact opportunities.

## Overall Result

The redesigned homepage presents MK Barber Shop as a professional men's grooming destination with a clearer service structure, more accessible appointment pathways and better-organised branch information. The improvements focus on reducing booking friction, supporting local search visibility and helping customers find the right grooming service and location.

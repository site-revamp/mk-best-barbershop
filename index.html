Welcome To MK

Where Your Style Gets Its Signature.

Established in 2016 on Al Wasl Street in Dubai, MK Barber Shop was built around a simple idea: helping every customer look and feel their best.

Our professional barbers combine international barbering experience with an understanding of Arabic styling to deliver grooming that reflects your individual personality.

From precision haircuts and fades to beard styling and facial treatments, discover a grooming experience designed around you.

Good To Know

Before You Visit.

A few useful details to help you plan your MK Barber Shop experience.

Where can I find MK Barber Shop?

MK Barber Shop has three listed Dubai locations: Dar Al Wasl Mall in Jumeirah 2, Meyan Mall in Umm Suqeim 2 and NH Collection Dubai The Palm.

What grooming services are available?

Published services include haircuts, Afro fades, beard shaving and styling, hair treatments and facial treatments. Specific availability may vary between locations.

How can I book an appointment?

You can use the official MK Barber Shop booking platform on Fresha to explore available services and appointment options.

Can I contact the shop directly?

Yes. You can call the published contact number +971 4 335 3223 or use the official contact page to reach the business.

Does MK Barber Shop provide children's haircuts?

The official website also features a children's haircut gallery. Contact the relevant branch to confirm current availability.
